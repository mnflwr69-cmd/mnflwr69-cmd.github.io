Good questions

Can you handle a show-day emergency?

That's the job. Backstage work is mostly emergencies handled calmly — split seams, dead zippers, last-minute fit problems. If you're booking wardrobe support for an event, tell me the date and the size of the act in the builder above.

How do headdress classes work?

You book a seat, and your class kit — including the 3D-printed parts for that design — comes through me as part of the deal, so everything you need is waiting for you. Class day is assembly plus the hand work: fitting, finishing, and making the piece yours.

Do you take commissions?

Yes — custom clothing, headdresses, jewelry, and artwork. Commissions start with a conversation about what you want, your timeline, and your budget; I'll tell you straight whether I'm the right hands for it.

How fast can alterations be done?

It depends on the garment and the season — show season books up. Put your date in the request builder and I'll give you a real answer, not a guess.
